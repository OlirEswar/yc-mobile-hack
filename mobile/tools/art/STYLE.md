# Living World art style

All game art is generated by code in this folder. Nothing is hand-painted, so every change is a
code change and every run produces byte-identical PNGs.

```sh
node mobile/tools/art/generate.mjs            # everything (about 15 s)
node mobile/tools/art/generate.mjs buildings  # or: ground, buildings, characters, icons
node mobile/tools/art/preview.mjs             # composites into mobile/tools/art/previews/
```

Node 22.6+ is required (the scripts import `shared/*.ts` directly with type stripping; Node
prints a harmless MODULE_TYPELESS_PACKAGE_JSON warning, silence it with `--no-warnings`). There are
no npm dependencies: `lib/image.mjs` holds the raster, a PNG encoder (indexed when an image has
at most 256 colours) and a decoder for the previews.

## Look

Modern high-resolution pixel art in a top-down 3/4 view, rendered at 1 world px = 1 art px with
nearest-neighbour scaling. Shackleton Colony sits near the lunar south pole, so the sun is always
low: light comes from the upper left and shadows fall long towards the lower right.

- **Ground** is a lit heightfield, not noise: rolling regolith, power-law craters with raised
  rims and bright ejecta on fresh ones, boulders, a raised rim along the top of the map, and the
  permanently shadowed crater (bottom left) that holds the ice wells. Slopes are shaded against
  the light and every pixel is ray-marched towards the sun for cast shadows, then quantised onto
  the palette ramps with a 4x4 ordered dither. Sunlit ground uses the warm regolith ramp, shadow
  the cold ramp, so shadows are cool blue-grey rather than black.
- **The core is graded**: around every footprint and along every route the ground is levelled,
  craters are kept out and a low berm of pushed regolith marks the edge.
- **Tubes** (routes with `surface: false`) are open-top pressurised corridors: a grated walkway
  between two walls, ribs every 32 px, teal lamps on the north wall. Horizontal runs show the inner
  face of the north wall and the outer face of the south wall, like the buildings.
- **Rover tracks** (`surface: true`) are compacted regolith with two tyre ruts, tread marks, low
  berms and red beacon posts.
- **Buildings** are open-top cutaways: wall tops, the inner face of the north wall (windows onto
  the stars, screens, shelves), floor, the outer face of the south wall. Doors are cut where the
  routes arrive. Furniture uses 3/4 boxes and cylinders (top face lit, front face one step darker,
  dark outline, short drop shadow). Building shadows are cast onto the ground image, not drawn in
  the building art.
- **Overlays** hold only what is above head height and stays light: dome ribs and glass at low
  alpha, awnings, grow-light bars, pendant lamps, a gantry, a washing line, a telescope tube,
  floodlight heads, the shuttle's tail fin.
- **Damaged** layers add scorch, branching cracks, debris, glass and small fires inside each
  building's outline.
- **Colonists** are chibi (head about 2.5 heads tall), 1 px dark selective outline (the
  neighbouring colour mixed 80% into ink), a soft foot shadow, lit from the upper left.

## Sizes and conventions

| Asset | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `ground.png` | 1280 x 1920 | MAP_WIDTH x MAP_HEIGHT, drawn at 0,0 |
| `buildings/<id>_{base,overlay,damaged}.png` | the footprint in `shared/mapLayout.ts` | drawn at the footprint's top-left |
| `characters.png` | 512 x 2400 | 32 x 40 frames; row = sprite in SPRITE_IDS order; column = direction (down, left, right, up) x 4 + frame; frame 0 stands; feet at (16, 37) |
| `portraits/<spriteId>.png` | 64 x 64 | the standing front frame's head and shoulders at 2x on an archetype-tinted backdrop |
| `icons/power_<type>.png` | 32 x 32 | one per PowerType |
| `icons/res_<id>.png` | 24 x 24 | one per ResourceId |
| `fx/grave.png` | 24 x 28 | mound at the bottom, sits on the colonist's last position |
| `fx/selection.png` | 16 x 14 | down chevron above the selected colonist |
| `fx/storm_overlay.png`, `fx/drought_overlay.png` | 128 x 128 | seamless tiles; storm streaks travel down-left to match the renderer's scroll |

Walled buildings share one shell (`lib/layout.mjs`): 16 px of headroom above the north wall for
masts and signs, 6 px wall tops, a 28 px north wall face, an 8 px south wall face, 4 px side
margins. Floors start 50 px below the footprint top. Stand areas in `shared/mapLayout.ts` are the
aisles between furniture rows, so a colonist's feet never land on a counter, rack or bed; working
colonists face up, towards the racks and counters along the north side of each aisle.

Character archetypes: farmer green coverall and sun hat; miner orange EVA suit, bubble helmet with
headlamp and life-support pack; engineer yellow jumpsuit, hard hat, goggles, tool belt; medic white
with red cross and teal gloves; scientist white lab coat over a coloured shirt, glasses, tablet;
merchant purple with gold trim and a satchel; guard dark blue armour, fitted helmet with a coloured
visor and rank stripe; official grey suit with a sash and pin; worker brown overalls, beanie, some
with aprons; civilian coloured tee and jeans. Variants change skin tone, hair colour and style
(short, long, curly, bun, bob, buzz) and accent colours.

## Map layout

The pressurised core is a grid of tubes (rows at y 704, 1064, 1424; columns at x 461, 640, 820).
Plaza, depot and medbay are hubs whose entry is their centre, with one door per side. Waypoints in
`ROUTE_WAYPOINTS` keep tubes orthogonal and carry the rover tracks around the buildings; the
painted tubes and tracks follow exactly the polylines the client walks.
